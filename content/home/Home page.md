

Life is a dance between  chaos and order. You have the ability to disrupt order and create chaos, but you can also bring moments of order into chaos, even if only temporarily.

To navigate life effectively, you need one foot in order—the familiar and known—and the other reaching into the unknown, searching for solid ground. This balance is necessary because order is always temporary.

To do this effectively, you need to gather information, evaluate it carefully, think it through, and take action based on what you’ve learned.
